    Your choice. Your questions.17 answers

    EgoAgent is used only with your written permission. You can change your mind.

    Your choice4 answers
    The consentA written consent you sign before anything begins.

    Your therapist sends you a written consent. It is a legal document: it says what is recorded and what is done with it, and it becomes part of your medical record. It asks you, one by one, whether your therapist may show the AI's analysis to a supervisor, to a colleague for a second opinion, or use it in research; otherwise no one else sees it. You sign it electronically, wherever you are, and can have a copy whenever you ask. Until you sign, nothing happens.

    Your sayRecording stops, and transcripts are deleted, whenever you say.

    Any session can go unrecorded if you ask, and the recorder can be stopped partway. You can revoke the consent at any time, in any words, by telling your therapist; recording then stops. And at any point in the treatment you can ask for your transcripts to be deleted, and they are. That is your right, not a request.

    What if my therapist says no?

    That is their decision, and they may decline. It is fair to ask why, and fair to ask again after some months, since answers change. You can also send them the therapist page, so they can judge it in their own language.

    Does this cost me anything?

    No. Your therapist subscribes. There is nothing for you to buy or sign up for.

    Your sessions5 answers
    The transcriptKept with your therapist’s notes, read by no one else, deleted after.

    It is a written transcript of the session, in the language you speak, kept with your therapist's notes and opened by no one but your therapist and the AI that analyzes it. The AI provider holds it under a binding business agreement that requires it to keep the material private and forbids using it for anything else. No person at EgoAgent reads it. You do not read it either; the reason is in the questions below. Six months after your last session it is deleted, unless you and your therapist have agreed a different length in writing.

    The notesThe analysis stays with your therapist, as notes always have.

    The AI's analysis goes to your therapist and stays with them, alongside their notes. What they make of it is theirs, as a therapist's notes have always been. That is what lets them think freely. So your words can be erased, and what your therapist made of them cannot; that has long been true of a therapist's notes, before there was any AI. What is new is the transcript, and that is yours to have deleted, and it does not outlive the treatment.

    Can I read my own transcript?

    Your therapist keeps it, and will usually advise against reading it. Therapy works in the session, in what passes between you and your therapist. A transcript to go back to pulls the attention out of that, toward the page; the words start to be examined instead of said. The transcript is also not only your words: it holds your therapist's, said to you in the session and belonging to their work, and no therapist can speak freely knowing they will be carried out of the room. So it stays with your therapist, as their notes always have. It exists for one purpose, to let the AI write an analysis your therapist can consider; on its own it would not help you. If you want to see it, talk with your therapist first. Your legal rights to your records are unchanged; your therapist can explain them. What you keep is the right to have it deleted, at any time.

    What happens when therapy ends?

    Your transcripts are kept for six months after your last session, in case you return, and then deleted; a different length only if you and your therapist agreed it in writing. Your therapist's notes stay with your therapist, as notes always have. If EgoAgent itself ever closes, everything it holds is deleted.

    My sessions are online, or not in English.

    Both are fine. On a call, the sound is recorded on the call instead of in the room, and everything else is the same. Sessions in any language the AI supports are written down word for word in that language; the written analysis your therapist reads can be in whatever language they prefer.

    Privacy & EgoAgent8 answers
    Does anyone else hear or read my sessions?

    No person at EgoAgent listens to your sessions or reads the transcript. The sound is transcribed and then permanently deleted. The transcript is analyzed by an AI model held under a binding business agreement that requires it to keep the material private and forbids any other use, and the analysis goes to your therapist alone. Whether a supervisor, a colleague, or research may ever see it is asked in your consent, question by question.

    Does it know my name?

    Yes. The transcript is the session as spoken, names included, and the AI analyzes it as spoken; nothing is blurred, because a blurred session cannot be analyzed well. What protects you is the agreement, not the blurring: the provider is bound to keep it private, no person at EgoAgent reads it, and the analysis goes to your therapist alone.

    Does the AI analyze my sessions instead of my therapist?

    No. Your therapist analyzes your sessions as they always have. The AI's analysis is something they can add to their own, session by session, and weigh as they see fit.

    Is this one of those AI apps that talk to you?

    No. It never speaks with you, and you never speak with it. It writes for your therapist, and only your therapist reads it.

    What is in the analysis?

    It looks for possible connections between your thoughts and your feelings as you expressed them in the session, and across sessions for what returns and what has changed, using ideas drawn from psychotherapy. It is written for your therapist. It is never a diagnosis, and never a score or rating of you or your therapist. Your therapist decides what in it, if anything, is useful.

    What if I say something dangerous?

    Your therapist hears it in the session and responds then, as they always have; their usual responsibilities in an emergency, or when the law requires a report, do not change because EgoAgent is used. The AI works only afterwards, on the transcript; it does not watch for danger, and it does not report, alert, or contact anyone.

    Who is it for?

    Adults in individual therapy, of the kind where what happens between you and your therapist, in the session, is itself the treatment. Your therapist registers with their National Provider Identifier, checked in the federal registry, and a sworn professional affirmation.

    Who made this?

    A practicing psychiatrist and psychoanalyst in New York City, for the work as therapists actually do it. If this page left a question unanswered, you can write to us from the Contact page and a person will reply.

    When you are ready, ask.

    It is a question, not a demand, and your therapist can say no. Or send this page to someone else in therapy who might want to ask.

    When the patient has left and something is still to be understood, bring it here.

    EgoAgent reads your session through the theory you select, with depth and angle as they apply.

    Across the treatment.

    A single session shows a moment; a treatment unfolds over many. EgoAgent reads each new session in light of the earlier ones, bringing back what returns, what shifts, and what develops. Patterns that are hard to hold in mind from week to week come into view.

    Another perspective, not another authority.

    EgoAgent’s reach is cognitive, not experiential. It has never met your patient and cannot feel what happens in the room. Its formulations are offered for you to consider, question, and develop.

    Practical details8 questions

    How does recording work?

    An app on your device, sound only, in the room or on the call. You press start and stop. The session arrives in that patient's workspace transcribed and filed by date; the audio exists only for transcription. Details are on the Privacy & Security page.

    What does my patient agree to?

    You send them a unique invitation link. They read the patient page, then complete the written permission — what is recorded, what is done with it, and what stays their choice. Nothing is recorded before it is completed, and any session can go unrecorded if they ask.

    Is the analysis ever automatic?

    No. The transcript arrives on its own; analysis waits until you select material and ask.

    Who can read the transcript and the consultations?

    The workspace is built as yours, and the patient page states the same boundary from their side. The precise access architecture is set out on the Privacy & Security page.

    What about deletion and retention?

    The rules are stated in the patient consent and on the Privacy & Security page, and your own material can be exported before anything is removed.

    Can I bring my own literature?

    Yes. Upload a PDF and bring it into a consultation. Sources are shown collapsed, marked by pool, and only when they support the point.

    Who can register?

    Licensed therapists in the United States, individually verified. The subscription belongs to you, not to a practice or an institute.

    Which patients and treatments?

    Adults in individual therapy, in work where what happens between you and the patient in the session is itself the treatment. Registration handles the exclusions; the patient page carries the same scope.
