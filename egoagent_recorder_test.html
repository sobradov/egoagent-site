The six tests

  1. Fifty minutes with the screen kept on. Put the phone on its charger.
  2. Start, lock the screen, wait two minutes, unlock, then stop.
  3. Start, open another app for one minute, come back, then stop.
  4. Start, and ask someone to call you. Answer or decline, come back, then stop.
  5. Add this page to the Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon, and run test 2 again.
  6. With a second person, made-up conversation, phone on the table between you. Listen afterwards: are both voices clear?
